## Market briefing
Vendors started shipping their playbooks inside the AI assistant rather than beside it: MCP servers can now carry skills, long-running tasks and sign-off prompts into Claude, and a cold-email vendor launched its own model to rank prospects before anything is sent.

## Fact vs interpretation
**Fact:** Nov 24 Nooks launched Org Charts and Warm Intros; Gong became available in Korean. Nov 24 Anthropic put Skills over MCP in public beta and made Tasks and Elicitation GA (HubSpot, ZoomInfo among users); Claude artifacts became installable as desktop apps (beta). Nov 24 Instantly launched its Pulse sales model; OpenAI published a customer story on Clay's GPT-6 use and ChatGPT/Codex plugins; OpenAI reported GPT-6 protein-design results; Meta put Muse in Instagram DMs with Walmart checkout. Salesforce, Outreach, Salesloft and Monaco announced nothing new of their own Nov 24–25.

**Interpretation:** With skills shipped by the data vendor itself, the workflow that used to live in a sales tool's UI can now arrive inside the rep's assistant, so differentiation shifts to the data and the playbook rather than the screen. Instantly and Nooks are both betting that proprietary activity data (replies, calls, relationships) is what a general model cannot replicate.
