## Market briefing
Sales context is being opened up to outside agents, and model vendors now want to know exactly who those agents serve. Zoom turned its meeting record into a layer any agent can query, while OpenAI and Anthropic both moved to identify the end customers behind apps built on their models.

- Meeting data becomes agent infrastructure: on Oct 21 Zoom introduced a Unified Context Layer that links meetings, phone, chat and documents, plus MCP servers and Search & Ask APIs (available now) so Claude, Codex, Copilot, Gemini, Slackbot and other MCP agents can query it. My Notes Workflows will turn meeting summaries into follow-up work in Salesforce and Zoom Chat, with ready-made sales workflows (expected October). [Zoom / Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261021498160/en/Zoom-Advances-Its-AI-Strategy-With-a-New-Unified-Context-Layer-That-Helps-Turn-Meeting-Conversations-Into-Completed-Work)
- Apps built on frontier models now carry end-customer identity duties: OpenAI's updated Usage Policies (effective Nov 20) require routers and gateways that offer direct model access to get OpenAI's approval, and it is introducing SafetyIDs to tell end users apart in investigations. Anthropic launched user profiles (Oct 20) and, on Oct 21, enforcement status fields showing whether one of your end customers has been restricted or banned. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/broad-access-through-consistent-standards/) · [Claude Platform release notes](https://docs.claude.com/en/release-notes/api)
