## Market briefing
The fight over AI agents is moving from what they can do to what it costs and who controls them inside the systems they touch. Salesforce is negotiating with partners over its plan to bill every agent call into the CRM, while ServiceNow shipped a personal enterprise agent whose actions are governed by IT policy and enforced in the runtime.

- Metering agent access to the CRM meets pushback: Salesforce plans to charge Flex Credits for every successful MCP or API call made by a registered agent (a "Headless Platform Interaction"; rate still TBA, 30 days' notice before billing). On Oct 21 Salesforce Ben reported that senior Salesforce stakeholders opened talks with partners after a backlash; the asks are more predictable agent pricing, value beyond plain MCP access, and room for AI-assisted org setup and support. No change has been announced. [Salesforce Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-opens-talks-with-partners-over-ai-agent-billing-concerns/) · [Salesforce Help: agent billing](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=005360285&language=en_US&type=1)
- Agent governance moves into the runtime: ServiceNow made Arc GA on Oct 21, a personal long-running agent across ServiceNow, email and Teams that can build its own integrations, with actions governed by IT policy and enforced in NVIDIA OpenShell. It is included at no extra cost in Foundation, Advanced and Prime SKUs. AI Passport revocation in software and silicon comes in a future release. [ServiceNow newsroom](https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-delivers-industry-first-personal-enterprise-agent-and-policy-enforcement-with-NVIDIA-extending-AI-governance-across-software-and-silicon/default.aspx) · [NVIDIA newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/openshell-reference-architecture)
- AI that runs whole GTM programs, not single tasks, is getting funded: Different came out of stealth on Oct 20 with a $48M seed led by General Catalyst. It plans, launches and optimizes B2B demand-gen campaigns across ads, email, landing pages and answer-engine content, with marketers approving everything before it goes live. Customer results are company claims. [Different / PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/different-emerges-from-stealth-with-48m-for-ai-that-runs-demand-generation-for-lean-b2b-marketing-teams-302913497.html)
