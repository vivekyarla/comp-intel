## Market briefing
AI-native CRMs are now going directly after Salesforce installs: Lightfield launched an enterprise edition with a 30-day migration and a buy-out of existing CRM contracts, and Dreamteam raised $53M for a CRM that captures email and meetings automatically and runs agents on top.

- Nov 16: Lightfield for Enterprise adds SSO, SCIM, per-object and per-record permissions, and custom objects. Agents, automations and API keys act with the permissions of the person they act for, and each AI-filled field can be set to Off, Suggest or Auto. Lightfield's own team migrates customers off Salesforce in 30 days or less (average six days), keeps two-way Salesforce sync during the move, credits the months left on a Salesforce or HubSpot contract, and does not charge until the agreed scope is delivered. [Lightfield (GlobeNewswire)](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/16/3403767/0/en/lightfield-launches-an-enterprise-ready-alternative-to-salesforce-with-migration-in-30-days-or-less.html)
- Nov 16: Dreamteam, founded by former Freshworks executives, raised $53M (seed led by Together Fund, Series A led by Accel). Its CRM builds a shared “Company Brain” from emails and meetings, and four built-in agents research prospects, draft follow-ups, flag deal risk and configure workflows. The company reports 100+ customers in three months of selling, including a Salesforce replacement at DMS Health. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/16/freshworks-alums-raise-53m-to-take-on-salesforce-hubspot-with-ai-native-crm/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/16/goodbye-manual-work-ai-native-crm-startup-dreamteam-raises-53m-to-autonomously-push-deals-forward/)

**Fact:** Nov 16 Lightfield launched Lightfield for Enterprise with a 30-day Salesforce migration and CRM contract buy-out. Nov 16 Dreamteam announced $53M for an AI-native CRM. Nov 16 Deloitte Digital launched Converge Sales and Converge Marketing. Nov 13 OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet. None of the seven core watchlist companies announced a new launch, funding, M&A or pricing change Nov 13–16.

**Interpretation:** Switching cost is becoming the main battleground in CRM: newer vendors are absorbing migration labor and existing contract terms, the two things that kept large teams on Salesforce. Agent permissions that mirror the acting user, as in Lightfield and ChatGPT Wallet, are turning into a baseline expectation for any agent that writes to records or spends money.
