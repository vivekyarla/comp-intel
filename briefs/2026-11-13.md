## Market briefing
Revenue tools are turning their data and actions into agent tools, and enterprise buyers are getting more say over where those agents run and how their usage is metered: Outreach's November release lets AI clients build whole sequences over MCP and splits credit usage into eight categories, and Anthropic now lets companies run Managed Agents entirely inside their own infrastructure.

- Nov 12: Outreach's MCP server adds 11 tools, so an AI client can create and edit a sequence end to end, read call logs and Kaia scorecards, and search templates, all under the user's existing role permissions. MCP connectors can now authenticate with an org-wide or per-user API key, and the AI metering dashboard now breaks credit usage into eight categories instead of two. The MCP features require the Amplify add-on. [Outreach release notes](https://support.outreach.io/support/solutions/articles/159000437513-outreach-product-release-notes-november-2026)
- Nov 12: Anthropic opened a private beta of self-hosted Claude Managed Agents. The customer runs the agent harness, sandboxes, credentials and session history in its own cloud or data center and picks where model calls go (Anthropic, Bedrock, Google, Microsoft Foundry or its own gateway); every session is logged and exportable over OpenTelemetry, and hosts can be allow-listed. [Claude](https://claude.com/resources/articles/self-hosted-claude-managed-agents) · [Claude docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/managed-agents/self-hosted)
- Nov 12: Microsoft said Copilot Workspaces will drop the separate Researcher, Analyst and Designer agent picker in favor of one agentic chat that applies those skills based on the request, starting in Frontier preview in December. [Microsoft 365 Message Center MC1550540](https://message.cengizyilmaz.net/message/mc1550540-microsoft-365-copilot-copilot-workspaces-is-replacing-standalone-agent-selection-with-agentic-chat)

## Fact vs interpretation
**Fact:** Nov 12 Outreach began shipping its November release with 11 new MCP server tools, API-key MCP connectors and eight AI credit categories. Nov 12 Salesforce released open-weight SOV-Transcribe-1. Nov 12 Anthropic launched self-hosted Claude Managed Agents in private beta. Nov 12 Microsoft announced Copilot Workspaces will replace standalone agent selection with agentic chat starting December. Nov 12 Jensen Huang resigned as NVIDIA CEO. Nov 12 Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Nov 12–13 Bloomberg reported Anthropic's IPO about six times oversubscribed. Nov 12 OpenAI launched Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory.

**Interpretation:** With Outreach, Gong and Salesforce all exposing write actions over MCP within a week, the sales engagement tool is increasingly a back end that outside agents drive, so control is shifting to whoever owns the agent and the permissions model. Itemized credit reporting and self-hosting both answer the same buyer question about cost and control, and are likely to become standard asks in enterprise GTM evaluations.
