## Market briefing
AI tools are moving from per-seat licenses to shared, metered budgets: GitHub now includes Copilot with every GitHub Enterprise seat and meters usage against one enterprise budget, and Claude Enterprise lets admins give each team a single pooled monthly budget. Governed workflow actions also keep moving into assistants, with ServiceNow's plugin now live in ChatGPT.

- Claude Enterprise smart reports, in-app surveys and pooled group budgets became generally available on Oct 28. Reports group a team's sessions into workstreams with spend and friction per workstream and recommend recurring workflows to turn into skills; budget alerts go to billing admins at 50%, 75%, 95% and 100%. [Claude](https://claude.com/resources/articles/smart-reports-surveys-pooled-budgets)
- From Oct 28, eligible GitHub Enterprise Cloud customers can get Copilot for every licensed user, with AI usage metered against the enterprise budget. Shared Copilot Automations, team-owned workflows with prompts, triggers and allowed tools, are rolling out and bill to the repository owner. [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-28-github-enterprise-now-includes-access-to-github-copilot/) · [GitHub Changelog (Automations)](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-28-build-shared-workflows-for-your-team-with-copilot-automations/)
- OpenAI and ServiceNow expanded their partnership on Oct 28. A ServiceNow MCP plugin is in the OpenAI marketplace now, letting users update ServiceNow records from ChatGPT while ServiceNow's Action Fabric applies each customer's existing rules and permissions. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-servicenow-partnership-expansion/)
