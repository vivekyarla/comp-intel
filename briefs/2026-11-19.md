## Fact vs interpretation
**Fact:** Nov 19 Clay launched a global agent in open beta. Nov 18 Attio relaunched its ChatGPT plugin, Outreach added MCP write tools and partner connectors, Monaco offered 10 ANT shares per November sign-up, and Benioff said 226K custom agents are deployed in Slack. Nov 18 Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 ($81.34B) with trading today; Google released Gemini 4 Neon; OpenAI added ChatGPT for Accounting. Gong, Nooks and Salesloft announced nothing of their own Nov 18–19.

**Interpretation:** Vendors are hedging: they expose data and write actions to ChatGPT so they are not shut out of the assistant layer, while building their own agents on context a general assistant lacks (Clay's Knowledge Hub and Audiences) and charging for agent work by usage.
