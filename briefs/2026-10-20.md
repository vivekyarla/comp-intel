## Market briefing
Agent software is being repriced and re-plumbed around metered work. Notion folded all AI usage into one credit pool with a cost-routing default and promised per-agent spend limits, while quoting vendor Vendavo is opening its CPQ to calls from other agents, with field-level entitlements and an audit trail on every approval.

- Credits, routing and spend controls become the agent pricing model: Notion (Oct 19) moved all Notion AI usage onto a new credit system, made its Auto router the default (the company says results are similar to Opus 5.5 and Astra at about 90% lower average cost across 20+ tasks), gave agents their own computer and browser for tasks up to 24 hours, and announced an AI Command Center (coming soon) for usage by person and agent, spending limits and model allow-lists. [Notion release notes](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-10-19)
- Deal desks become agent-callable services: Vendavo's AI Deal Desk Assistant (Oct 20) answers renewal and pricing questions inside the quote, and Vendavo is extending it to draft quotes from call transcripts, run approvals against margin rules, and accept agent-to-agent quote requests from CRM, with field-level entitlements and audited approvals. Productivity and margin figures are company claims. [Vendavo / PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vendavo-launches-ai-deal-desk-assistant-bringing-deal-intelligence-directly-into-the-quoting-workflow-302911681.html)
- Inbound follow-up is where sales agents are landing in production: at Salesforce's AIforce Sydney event (Oct 20), Lawpath said it runs Agentforce SDR agents with a sales team of about 25 on an all-inbound model, for membership win-backs and enquiries left uncontacted for five days; voice agents are still in testing. [Salesforce ANZ newsroom](https://www.salesforce.com/au/news/stories/aiforce-world-tour-essentials-sydney-2026/)
