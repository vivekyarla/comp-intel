## Market briefing
Approval policies and data controls became the product on Monday: Anthropic shipped agents that review, fix and approve pull requests under a team-written policy, and opened a Bedrock route that keeps retained prompts for its top models in the customer's own AWS account.

- Nov 23: Claude Ship (beta, Team and Enterprise, GitHub) pairs Code Review with PR Steward, which fixes failing checks and review feedback but is designed not to approve or merge, and Claude Approvals, which approves only what a repository policy file allows; Anthropic recommends starting in observe-only mode.
