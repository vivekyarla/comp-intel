## Fact vs interpretation
**Fact:** Nov 17 OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Sales with 30+ partner plugins. Nov 17 Salesforce and Microsoft announced Copilotforce, with a 38+ skill Salesforce plugin for Copilot in public preview. Nov 17 Outreach released Outreach Skills for ChatGPT and Clay relaunched its ChatGPT MCP. Nov 17 Microsoft extended Agent 365 identity and spend tracking to non-Microsoft agents. Nov 17 NVIDIA reported $115.8B revenue and said Huang will not return. Nov 18 CNBC reported Anthropic may upsize its IPO. Gong, Nooks, Salesloft and Monaco announced nothing of their own Nov 17–18.

**Interpretation:** The assistant layer (ChatGPT, Copilot) is becoming a main place sellers do CRM and engagement work, so sales vendors are competing on packaged skills, data access and permission models inside those assistants rather than on their own UI.
