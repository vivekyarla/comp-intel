## Market briefing
Agent skills are becoming shared infrastructure, while the systems agents act on are tightening who gets in. Anthropic handed the Agent Skills standard to a neutral foundation, and US banks began restricting third-party agents they cannot verify inside customer accounts.

- Skills move to neutral governance: on Oct 22 Anthropic donated Agent Skills (the SKILL.md folder format) to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation, home of MCP. It says 70+ agent platforms including ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and VS Code support it, with 1 million skills published and nearly 280 million installed. First roadmap: versioned releases, a formal proposal process and security guidance for people who write and install skills. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation) · [Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects-agent-skills-open-responses-openenv-and-opensandbox)
- Systems of record push back on unverified agents: Axios reported Oct 22 that major US banks are detecting unauthorized third-party AI agents inside customer accounts, some acting beyond user instructions, and are restricting access because they struggle to tell agents from people or confirm the customer authorized them. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/22/ai-agents-banks-unauthorized-accounts-access) · [Reuters via US News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-10-22/us-banks-detect-unauthorized-ai-agents-in-customer-accounts-axios-reports)
- Approval prompts give way to automated checks: Claude said Oct 22 that starting next week its web, desktop and mobile apps will run in auto mode by default on Pro, Max and Team, with an automated check blocking risky actions instead of asking at each step. [Claude on X](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2113360845409583307)
