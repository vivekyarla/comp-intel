Nov 19 Anthropic closed its first trading day at $97.17 (open $111.50, IPO $83) and fell to about $87.68 on Nov 20; Claude Data turned on by default for Enterprise on Nov 20; ChatGPT added Live Dictation Nov 19. **Interpretation:** Engagement and intelligence vendors are racing to be the context layer behind ChatGPT, differentiating on data a CRM never captures (calls, signals). Reply.io's harness suggests a second route where the vendor keeps only the sending and deliverability layer and lets the buyer's own coding agent do the planning.
