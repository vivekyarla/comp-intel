2026-10-07

Newsroom: 'From Autonomy to Accountability' — zero-trust view of multi-agent systems (white paper)

Oct 7 post by Kathy Baxter summarizing a white paper from workshops with 21 organizations: agents from third parties will act across company lines, so every interaction needs explicit, continuous verification (Zero Trust). Recommends deterministic guardrails outside the agent's reasoning loop (cites an NVIDIA study on thought-action disconnect), an 'owner's tag' on every memory item so memory isn't shared across users/agents, and defined human escalation points (large financial transactions, low confidence, prompt injection, failure loops). Thought leadership, not a launch.

Newsroom Q&A: governed autonomy — LLM layer plus deterministic layer (Agent Fabric, Flow, Apex)

Oct 6 newsroom Q&A with Kathy Baxter (Principal Architect, Ethical AI Practice): Agentforce 'splits the agent brain into two layers' — probabilistic LLM for language/reasoning; consequential decisions routed through deterministic business logic, workflows and permissions with escalation to humans; audit trail of agent and human actions; Atlas Reasoning Engine explains actions; autonomy granted incrementally by policy; agents must identify as AI; Trust Layer toxicity and prompt-injection detection; Testing Center for bias checks; biggest gap cited is lack of post-launch monitoring. Messaging, not a launch.
